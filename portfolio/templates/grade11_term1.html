Creating a Batik Motif with Transformations

What:
I created a batik motif in GeoGebra using reflections, rotations, functions, and other mathematical transformations.
Why:
The project helped me understand how mathematical rules can create symmetry and connect geometry with Indonesian batik design.
How:
I started with a basic motif, reflected and rotated it across the coordinate plane, then adjusted functions and coordinates to build a repeated pattern.
Conclusion:
Designing the motif made transformations more visual and helped me see how equations can become part of a creative composition.

Making Physics Understandable Through Video

What:
My group made a video explaining circular motion and how an object moves around a fixed centre or axis.
Why:
The goal was to explain circular motion clearly and connect concepts such as speed, radius, and direction to examples from everyday life.
How:
We planned the explanation, divided the topic into sections, recorded examples of rotation, and edited the footage into one instructional video.
Conclusion:
Creating the video helped me understand the topic well enough to explain circular motion using simple visuals and clear examples.
