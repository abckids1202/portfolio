Three projects from this term, covering mathematical transformations, rotational motion, and the structure of informative writing.

01

Mathematics — Batik Motif in GeoGebra

Mathematics
IMAGE PLACEHOLDERGeoGebra batik motifAdd your screenshot here later
What:
I designed a batik motif in GeoGebra by combining reflections, rotations, functions, and other transformations.
Why:
The project helped me see how mathematical rules can create a balanced visual pattern and connect geometry with Indonesian culture.
How:
I created a basic motif, reflected and rotated it across the coordinate plane, then used functions and careful adjustments to build the final composition.
Conclusion:
This activity made transformations easier to understand because I could immediately see how each equation changed the motif.
02

Physics — Spinning Motion Video

Physics
IMAGE PLACEHOLDERSpinning motion videoAdd a thumbnail or production photo here later
What:
My group made a video explaining spinning motion and how objects move around an axis.
Why:
The goal was to explain a physics concept clearly and connect the formulas to motion that we can observe in everyday life.
How:
We planned the scenes, divided the explanation into sections, recorded examples of rotation, and edited the footage into one instructional video.
Conclusion:
Making the video pushed me to understand spinning motion well enough to explain it with simple visuals and clear examples.
03

English — Pompeii Brochure

English
IMAGE PLACEHOLDERPompeii brochureAdd the brochure photos here later
What:
I created an informative brochure about Pompeii, focusing on its history, important places, and the story of the city.
Why:
The assignment helped me learn how an effective brochure presents information in a concise, engaging, and easy-to-navigate format.
How:
I studied the style and structure of example brochures, organized the information into sections, and combined short descriptions with visual elements.
Conclusion:
This project improved both my research and communication skills because I had to make historical information accessible to a reader.
