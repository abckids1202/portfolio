Physics

Rotary System

Physics: What?

?

We learned about circular motion, describing an object moving along a circle's circumference at constant speed.

?

We analyzed the difference between uniform circular motion and rotational kinematics.

?

Our objective was to create a physical rotary mechanism and count the rotational kinematics.

Physics: Why?

!

To bridge theoretical knowledge with practical application.

!

To understand how variables like radius and time affect the system's dynamics.

!

To practice calculating real-world measurements and analyzing data accuracy.

Rotational Formulas

Variable Formula Unit
Period (T) t / n Seconds (s)
Frequency (f) n / t or 1 / T Hertz (Hz)
Angular Velocity (ω) 2πf rad/s
Linear Speed (v) ωr m/s
Centripetal Acc (ac) v2 / r m/s2
Centripetal Force (Fc) m v2 / r Newton (N)

Physics: How?

➜

Constructed a rotary system using a dynamo, cardboard, and an electric circuit.

➜

Used a stopwatch to measure the time for 10 full rotations to calculate Period (T).

➜

Derived Frequency, Angular Velocity, and Centripetal Force using the collected data.

➜

Concluded by analyzing the relationship between radius and force required.
